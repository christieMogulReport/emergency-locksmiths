About this guide

Last updated November 18, 2026

The Emergency Locksmith Guide is a small, independent directory of plain-English help for people who need a locksmith right now: lockouts at home, in the car, or at a business, and rekeying after a break-in.

What we publish

  • Cost guides built from published price surveys and cost guides, with the source linked next to each number.
  • Scam warnings based on advice from the Federal Trade Commission, the Better Business Bureau, and state consumer protection offices.
  • Checklists you can use on the phone before anyone is dispatched.
  • Local listings, added only after we have checked a business’s own site or a public record. We do not make up businesses, phone numbers, or prices.

How we handle information

Hours, prices, and licensing rules change. Each page shows the date it was last updated. When a number is a national average, we say so. Please confirm the price with the locksmith before they begin work, and get it in writing.

We are not a locksmith, we do not dispatch anyone, and we cannot vouch for any business you find through a search engine. Nothing here is legal advice.

How the site may earn money

Some businesses may pay for a featured listing, or may give us a referral link. Any link like that will be labeled as an affiliate or sponsored link, and the disclosure appears at the bottom of every page. Payment does not change the scam checklist or the price information.

Corrections

Prices and rules change, so a page can go out of date. Check the “last updated” date at the top of each page, and treat anything older than a year with caution. This site is published under the Black Swan Sibyl brand.
